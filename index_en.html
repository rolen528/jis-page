About JIS

A Strong Bridge Connecting
Global Partnerships and
Manufacturing Innovation

Established in 2006, JIS is a specialized import-export trading company connecting Korea's excellent manufacturing technologies with global markets, starting with the export of precision injection and automotive molds to Japan.

We have built close collaborative relationships with various Korean manufacturers, identifying and supplying products with optimal quality and competitiveness to the Japanese market. Through strong partnerships with leading companies such as Ikegami Kanagata and ELMEX, we operate a reliable trading business.

Based on decades of accumulated field expertise, JIS is expanding beyond traditional manufacturing networks into advanced bio-industries through its affiliate "JSES", continuing our journey of innovation and growth.
